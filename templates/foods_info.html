About Healthy Foods:

Healthy food includes natural, fresh, and nutritious meals such as fruits, vegetables, whole grains,
nuts, and lean proteins. It gives your body the vitamins and energy it needs, helps improve digestion,
and supports long-term health. Eating healthy regularly makes you feel more energetic and reduces the risk
of diseases.

Positive effects:

  • Boosting energy and strength
  • Strengthening the immune system
  • Supporting digestion and gut health
  • Reducing risk of illnesses
  • Improving overall well-being

Negative effects:

  • Taking time to prepare meals
  • Requiring planning and consistency
  • Sometimes being more expensive
