About Fast Food:

Fast food is quick, convenient, and often very tasty, but it is usually high in calories, sugar, salt,
and unhealthy fats. It can be enjoyable sometimes, but eating it too often may lead to weight gain and
health problems. Fast food is best consumed in moderation as a treat, not a daily habit.

Positive effects:

  • Providing quick and easy meals
  • Saving time in busy situations
  • Being widely available
  • Offering strong taste and satisfaction

Negative effects:

  • Increasing calorie and fat intake
  • Contributing to weight gain
  • Providing low nutritional value
  • Negatively affecting long-term health
