About Fitness Food:

Fitness food is designed to support exercise and an active lifestyle. It is rich in protein, healthy fats,
and complex carbohydrates that help build muscle, increase strength, and improve recovery after workouts.
Common fitness foods include chicken, eggs, rice, oats, and vegetables, which provide clean energy for the
body.

Positive effects:

  • Supporting muscle growth and strength
  • Enhancing recovery after workouts
  • Providing clean and stable energy
  • Improving physical performance
  • Supporting active lifestyle

Negative effects:

  • Becoming repetitive over time
  • Requiring discipline and planning
  • Sometimes being costly
